## EN announcement — 224 words

Marchland Oaths v1.5.1 is available as a portable Windows x64 release. Begin a frontier journey, explore its roads and settlements, trade supplies, build a homestead, and guide a sixty-objective campaign through the published edition's diplomatic and military routes. The illustrated title, readable controls, atlas, settings, and credits share the same restrained visual direction.

Choose English or Russian and switch languages during play. The soundtrack follows the journey with four distinct compositions: Stone Path to Dusk, Hearths Among the Hills, Steel and Rain, and Standards at Dawn. Oaths Remembered and The Last Watch accompany the endings. Separate music, effects, interface, and master controls let you set a comfortable mix.

The game runs offline after extraction and needs no account or live service. Authenticated saves, bounded validation, backups, and critical-data checks help detect ordinary corruption and editing. Previous published-build checks covered valid protected 1.4.0 and 1.5.0 profiles, their installation keys, and progress; back up the complete profile before upgrading. Iron-mode death remains terminal, while ordinary recovery retains its disclosed cost.

This is an unsigned portable Windows edition: normal Windows security warnings may appear. Android and store certification are outside this release. Automated tests cover defined scenarios on the available host; they do not promise every-device compatibility, universal absence of bugs, invulnerable protection, or AAA production scale. Review the included credits, licenses, and owner handoff before sharing.

После распаковки можно играть без сети, аккаунта и внешнего сервиса.
