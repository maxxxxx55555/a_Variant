# Premium presentation 1.3 design

The user authorizes autonomous redesign of both games, especially new type, premium buttons and more detailed characters/world, with real playthrough/fix iterations. They request no approval pauses and a two-line English final. This supersedes optional design review gates. Existing approved detailed financial/RPG art direction remains the starting point. No claim of GTA V production scale, photorealism or perfect security; actual runnable work determines acceptance.

Approaches considered: (1) change only colors/fonts, low risk but insufficient visible character improvement; (2) coherent new interface plus licensed higher-detail rigged humans and retained performant PBR scenery, selected; (3) new photoreal engine/world/rig pipeline, high regression and scope risk. Preserve existing mechanics, content, audio identities, protected profiles and collision footprints.

Finance identity: modern private-bank instrument, midnight Oxford #111e2e, satin blue #263f56, platinum #dce3e9, muted steel #9faebd and small champagne #d3b78b accents. Onest body/utility, restrained Unbounded display. A genuinely new layered button surface with visible hover, press, disabled and keyboard-focus states; no fake button labels or decorative counters. A fictional detailed life portrait changes with age while actual balances remain primary. Existing full-screen endings remain playable.

RPG identity: cinematic frontier chronicle, charcoal #1c2328, slate #303d46, pewter #b6c2c5, parchment-white #e7e6df and restrained oxidized burgundy #734c4e. Golos Text body/utility with Literata display. Consistent tactile control surfaces across title, council, inventory, settings and ending. More anatomically proportioned licensed skinned characters with modular clothes/hair, preserved walking/idle animation, weapon visibility and combat ownership. Retain existing PBR world, refine atmosphere/readability without obscuring play.

Controls must keep readable Russian/English, visible focus and disabled states, touch targets, keyboard navigation, layout bounds and real transactions. Motion must not alter hitboxes or create layout jumps. Character resources/materials/animations share caches; only nearby figures render at full detail, gameplay colliders remain unchanged. Every new imported asset requires license/provenance and shipped digests. Generated 2D artwork must not be represented as playable 3D.

Acceptance: visible before/after improvement in real new captures; typography has Cyrillic coverage; controls work at existing target resolutions; new humans render and animate without floating weapons or broken skins; no unexpected engine/script errors; full source regression/security suites, both exact native 60-objective routes, financial win/loss and exact Android preview touch/lifecycle/recovery pass. Measure real renderer performance. Keep previous releases immutable and back up whole profiles. Publish versioned source/builds/QA only after passing.
