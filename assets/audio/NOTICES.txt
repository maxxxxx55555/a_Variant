# Audio provenance

## Background music

The shipped music was generated with Google Gemini / Lyria on 2026-10-24 for this game using original instrumental descriptions. It is AI-generated music, not a human performance. Prompts, source filenames, SHA-256 checksums and ordinary mastering steps are recorded in music-provenance.json. Original downloads are retained with the release QA artifacts.

Generated music is supplied as generated output under the account holder's applicable [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Generative AI use policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy). It is **not** represented as CC0, exclusively copyrighted, or a cleared third-party recording. Google does not claim ownership of original content generated by its services, as described in those terms.

## Sound effects

Only the synthesized sound effects created by tools/generate_audio.py are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. No downloaded sound-effect samples were used. NumPy (BSD-3-Clause) is a build-time dependency and is not distributed with the games.
